💎 Symbol Layer — Programming Symbols & Inward Rolls #

Activated by holding Right Thumb Primary Key (RTU0: Space / Symbol)

The crown jewel of the Glorious Engrammer configuration: an entire layer dedicated to programming symbols, arranged so that the most common multi-character operators (();, ->, =>, |>, !=, ~/, ${, #{) are effortless inward finger rolls on the left hand while the right thumb holds the Symbol layer key.

Highlight Group:
Interactive:
Select an example above or click any bigram chip below to visualize the finger roll sequence.
Red ① = Hold Layer Key  ·  Yellow ②③④ = Tap Sequence & Roll Direction
  • \ (LTU3) is on the left thumb for escaping any other symbol without moving your fingers off the home block.
  • For snake_case, _ (L23) sits directly on the home row (index finger).
  • For assignment, = (L22) is on the home row middle finger because it is ubiquitous in programming.
  • For function calls, ();, (L02..L05) sits along the top row, rolling inward from ring → middle → index → inner index.
  • Curly braces {} (L12, L13) sit on the upper row wrapped by square brackets [] (L11, L14).
  • Angling arrows ->, =>, <=, <- and functional pipes |> roll naturally along the lower/home rows.

Spacegrams — Typing Spaces, Newlines & Corrections Inside the Symbol Layer #

When typing two symbols separated by a space (such as a = b or x != y), releasing the right-thumb Symbol key just to tap Space and then re-holding Symbol causes timing misfires known as "spacegrams". To eliminate this, the right-hand home row provides dedicated Bksp (R22), Tab (R23), Space (R24), and Enter (R25) keys directly inside the Symbol layer! Base Layer Affinity & Shift-Morph #

  • \ (LTU3) is on the exact same thumb key as Esc on the Base Layer: both escape things.
  • ~ (L30) is on the exact same pinky key as OSM(LShift) on the Base Layer: both invert things.
  • On the Base Layer itself, the lower-thumb ( (LTL2) and ) (LTL3) keys Shift-Morph into < and > when combined with Shift (parang_left / parang_right).

Right-Handed Mouse Usage#

Editing and selection shortcuts are mirrored onto the left hand (Cut on L15, Copy on L25, Paste on L35, Undo/Redo on LTL1/LTL2, and SelAll/SelLine/SelWord on L31..L33) so you can keep your right hand on your mouse while cutting, copying, pasting, and undoing entirely with your left hand.

Home-Row Arrow Keys (Left, Up, Down, Right)#

The right-hand home row (R21..R24) arranges the arrows as Left, Up, Down, Right so each finger is responsible for a single cardinal direction, with Home, PgUp, PgDn, End (R31..R34) directly beneath them.

Word & Line Selection Engine + Pinching#

OS-aware selection triggers (SelWord, ExtWord, SelLine, ExtLine, SelNone, and SelAll) sit on the thumb and upper-right clusters opposite Cut/Copy/Paste (R10, R20, R30) — tap SelWord to highlight the current word, tap ExtWord (or hold Shift + SelWord) to extend selection word-by-word, and pinch to copy.

🔒 Lower / OS, System / RGB, Base Win & Typing Layers #

Usage Example:
Select an example above to explore Layer Locking, Dynamic macOS/Windows Switching, Wireless/RGB Controls, or HRM-Free Typing mode.
Lower / OS (L7) Layer Locks & Numpad Row: Hold LT(7, Ins) (LTU2/RTU2) or MO(7) (LTL0/RTL0) for TO(1..6) locks, TG(9) Typing toggle, and OS_TOGG.
Sys / RGB (L6) RGB, Wireless & Battery: Hold LT(6, Enter) (RTU3) for LED Next/Prev/Togg, BatLvl, BT_PAIR, RF_MODE, EngySt, and OS-aware LockScr.
Base Win (L8) Dynamic CAGS ↔ GACS: Tap OS_TOGG on L6/L7 (or chord B + OSM(LShift)) to swap Ctrl ↔ Gui home-row mods & OS shortcuts while keeping your custom thumbs.
Typing (L9) HRM-Free Plain QWERTY: Toggle TG(9) on L7 (L06/LTL1) for zero-hold plain alphas during fast prose or gaming; chord 3 thumbs (TO(0)) or tap TG(9) (R06) to return.
